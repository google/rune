**Risks:**
- COVERAGE BYPASS (the headline risk): if desugar-emitted members are allowed to skip Check Phase-2 body typecheck (a tempting 'already generated, don't re-check' shortcut), then secret propagation and ref/unref checks — once implemented — silently never run on relation-generated code. The codebase already partially defers (uncalled generated methods, typechecker.rn:4609-4630); widening that into a full bypass is the single most damaging regression.
- LOSING THE `generated` FLAG: it is the only marker distinguishing synthesized from user code and is load-bearing for toString-recursion avoidance (info.generatedFieldNames -> noPrint, function.rn:632-636) and for any future 'compiler-synthesized, apply policy X' logic. A new desugar copy path that forgets to propagate statement.generated (currently preserved at statement.rn:186) breaks codegen and erases future-policy hooks.
- SHALLOW/PARTIAL Expr COPY ALIASING: expr.copy() shares Expr.val by reference (expr.rn:775) and does not copy datatype (769-770 TODO). Today secret lives on the shared Bigint (bigint.rn) so a future in-place secret mutation would contaminate sibling relation instantiations — the Lyric deep-copy contamination footgun, here for the secret bit. A future secret-on-datatype bit would vanish on every transformer copy.
- ELIDING ref/unref AS DEAD: because they are no-ops now (typechecker.rn:3723-3724), a desugar/optimize pass could drop them as unused, removing the future refcount checker's input and silently voiding the memory-safety contract Rune must keep (Lyric dropped it; Rune must not).
- PRE-FOLDING secret/reveal AT DESUGAR: since codegen treats them as identity (expr.rn:2403-2410), desugar might fold them early — erasing the secrecy boundary before a constant-time mode or a Secret/Reveal arm in expr() can observe it.
- SILENT POLICY DRIFT on uncalled-generated-method errors (typechecker.rn:4609-4630): relocating expansion can flip Rune between 'defer until called' and 'never check' / 'check up front' without anyone intending it, changing whether generated secret/ref misuse is caught at declaration or only on a reachable call.
- TWO SECRET REPRESENTATIONS: secret appears both as ExprType.Secret (expr.rn:74/284) and as a Call to ident 'secret' (the form codegen folds, expr.rn:2401-2410). Desugar must canonicalize without dropping either, or a future secret-aware Check sees only one form.

**Open questions:**
- Should Rune keep Lyric's defer-errors-on-uncalled-generated-methods (typechecker.rn:4609-4630) or adopt the CLAUDE.md fail-loud preference with an up-front structural check on generated bodies? This decides whether generated secret/ref misuse is caught at declaration or only when reachable from a call.
- Where will secret propagation ultimately live — a secret bit added to the Type union (typeclasses.rn:836) and/or Datatype (datatype.rn:40), or remain value-level on Bigint (bigint.rn:35)? Determines exactly what expr.copy()/desugar must carry through, and whether step-2's datatype-copy fix is on the critical path.
- Will ref/unref become real Check obligations (replacing the no-ops at typechecker.rn:3723-3724) before or after the desugar relocation? If after, the guardrail must guarantee the nodes survive desugar untouched in the interim.
- Does the C backend's identity-fold of secret/reveal (expr.rn:2403-2410) need to become secret-aware (constant-time), and if so must desugar preserve a distinct Secret node rather than a Call(ident 'secret') so the boundary is still visible at codegen?
- Are `trusted` and `permanent` modeled anywhere in the bootstrap yet? I found no handling — they scope where raw ref/unref is legal and which classes opt out of reclamation; their absence may mean the ref/unref guardrail can only be partially specified until they exist.
- Should a desugar-output invariant be enforced mechanically (e.g. an assertion that every node reaching codegen with .generated=true also passed Phase-2), or is a fixture-diff in step-10 sufficient as the regression net?
